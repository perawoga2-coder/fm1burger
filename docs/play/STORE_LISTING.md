**Full description** (EN)
```
Burger Studio is a groovebox for your phone or tablet, made by Maker Hip.

• Sample tracks with a step sequencer: record from the mic, import WAV, chop and play
• Drum pads, a bass synth and an analog-style synth, an FM bank
• Song parts (Q1 to Q6), a song builder, live part recording
• A mixer with effects, a master limiter, mix and stems recording
• Plays together with the M-VAVE FM-1 over USB or Bluetooth MIDI: tempo sync, send samples to its slots,
  and VACUUM the whole song from Maker Hip's STACK firmware into the app
• → MIX: hand a song to 16 Track, Maker Hip's multitrack recorder, as stems
• Backup and restore everything in one file

Free. No account, no ads, no tracking: your music stays on your device.
```

## App content (the forms)

- **Privacy policy**: the URL above.
- **Ads**: No, the app has no ads.
- **App access**: All functionality is available without special access (no login, no code).
- **Content rating** (IARC questionnaire): category "All other app types"; no violence, no sexual content, no
  profanity, no drugs, no gambling; users do not interact or share content with each other inside the app; no
  location sharing; no purchases. Expected: Everyone / 3+.
- **Target audience**: 13+ (or 18+). Not "designed for children".
- **News app**: No. **Government app**: No. **Financial features**: None. **Health**: No.
- **Data safety**: *Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?* **No.** (The app has no
  internet permission and sends nothing anywhere. Microphone audio is recorded only when the user presses record and
  stays on the device; Google's form counts only data that leaves the device.)
- **Permissions declared**: RECORD_AUDIO (record into tracks), BLUETOOTH_SCAN with neverForLocation and
  BLUETOOTH_CONNECT (Bluetooth MIDI), HIGH_SAMPLING_RATE_SENSORS (keyboard velocity). No sensitive-permission
  declaration form is expected for these.
