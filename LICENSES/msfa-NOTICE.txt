The FM voice renderer of Burger Studio (src/main/java/com/example/fm1burger/FmRender.kt and
tools/fm6.py) follows the structure of the DX7 engine in "Music Synthesizer for Android"
(https://github.com/google/music-synthesizer-for-android):

Taken from it, re-written in floating point: the 32 algorithm routing table, the envelope
level / rate arithmetic and its tables (level, velocity, exponential key scaling), the
coarse / fine / detune frequency formulas and the DX7 voice unpacking layout.

The "MakerHip FM" voices (tools/make_fm_bank.py, src/main/assets/fmbank) are original to
Burger Studio.
